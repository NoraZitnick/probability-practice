    Summary

    Independent

    Two gates with a shuffler between them. Passing A doesn’t change the chance at B.

    P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

    Dependent

    Two gates with no shuffler. A marble keeps its position, so passing A changes the chance at B.

    P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B | A)

    Mutually exclusive

    Two doors side by side in one wall. A marble can’t fall through both.

    P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)

    Not mutually exclusive

    Two doors that overlap. A marble in the overlap falls through both.

    P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) – P(A and B)

    And: multiply. Stacked gates ask whether a marble passes A and B. If passing A changes the chance at B, multiply by P(B | A).

    Or: add. Doors in one wall ask whether a marble passes A or B. If the doors overlap, subtract the overlap so it isn’t counted twice.
